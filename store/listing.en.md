## Detailed description

Save articles to read later — on the train, on a plane or just in the evening. The text, your highlights and notes live in your browser, not on a remote server: no sign-up, and nobody can shut your library down.

Features

• One-click saving: the toolbar icon, Alt+Shift+S or "Save page for later" in the context menu. Save a link from its context menu, or several selected tabs at once.
• Clean article text without ads or menus, with title, author, date and reading time. Images are kept for offline reading (per-article limit is configurable).
• Reader: font, size, line spacing, line width, light, sepia and dark themes. Your reading position is remembered for every article.
• Highlights in four colors with notes. Copy them as Markdown.
• Library: unread, favorites, archive, tags, full-text search, sorting, list or card view, bulk actions and keyboard shortcuts.
• Move from other services: import Pocket exports (HTML and CSV), Omnivore exports (ZIP with text and highlights), Instapaper (CSV), browser bookmarks or a plain list of links. Articles without text can be fetched later.
• No lock-in: a complete JSON backup, an HTML bookmarks file and Markdown files for Obsidian or any notes app. The extension reminds you to make a backup.
• A 30-day trash: anything deleted by mistake is easy to restore.

How to use

1. Open an article and click the extension icon (or press Alt+Shift+S).
2. Read it from the library or the popup — online or offline.
3. Moving from another service? Settings → Import → choose your export file.

Privacy

No account, ads, analytics or servers. A page is read only after your action. Access to other websites is requested only while loading text for saved links or saving several tabs, and is removed right after.

Free and open source (MIT). This extension will never be sold.
